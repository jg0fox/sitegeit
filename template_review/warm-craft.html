Baked With Love Since 2012

Golden Crust Bakery started with a single brick oven and a family recipe for sourdough. Over the years we have grown, but the approach stays the same: real ingredients, patient hands, and no shortcuts. Every morning our team arrives before dawn to shape, proof, and bake so that everything you take home is as fresh as it gets. We are proud to be part of the Savannah community and grateful for every customer who walks through our door.
